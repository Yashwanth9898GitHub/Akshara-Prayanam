    Refund Policy

    What happens if you need to cancel an order, or if your eBook has a problem.

    Cancelling an order

    You can cancel your order at any time before we send you the eBook by messaging us on WhatsApp. If you have already paid, we will refund the full amount.

    Problems with your eBook

    If your PDF will not open, is incomplete, or is not the book you ordered, please message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of receiving it. We will send you a working copy, or refund you in full if we cannot fix the problem.

    Change of mind

    Because an eBook cannot be returned once it has been sent, we are unable to offer refunds for change of mind after delivery.

    How refunds are made

    • Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method, or another method we agree with you on WhatsApp.
    • We aim to process refunds within 7 working days of approval. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time.
