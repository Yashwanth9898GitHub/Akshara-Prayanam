    Privacy Policy

    How we handle the information you share with us.

    Akshara Prayanam respects your privacy. This page explains what information we receive, why we use it and the choices you have.

    Information this website collects

    This website does not have user accounts, does not use cookies for tracking and does not run analytics or advertising scripts. Searching and filtering books happens entirely in your browser.

    Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives standard technical information such as your IP address, as described in Google’s own privacy policy.

    Information you share on WhatsApp

    When you tap “Get This Book” or “Chat on WhatsApp”, WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it. If you message us, we receive the information you share, which may include:

    • your name and WhatsApp phone number;
    • the books you are interested in;
    • an email address, if you would like your eBook sent by email.

    How we use your information

    We use this information only to reply to you, confirm payment and send you your eBook. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

    Sharing

    We do not share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it. Payments are handled by the payment service you choose (for example UPI), under that service’s own privacy policy.

    Keeping and deleting information

    We keep order conversations only as long as needed for support and our records. You can ask us at any time to delete your conversation and details by messaging us on WhatsApp.

    Contact

    For any privacy question, message us on WhatsApp at +91 9848085505.

    We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
