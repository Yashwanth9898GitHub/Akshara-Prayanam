        Our story

        A journey that begins with a single letter

        Akshara (అక్షర) means a letter — the smallest piece of every story ever written. Prayanam (ప్రయాణం) means a journey.

        Together they hold a simple belief: every book is a road. Some lead home to familiar words and memories; others take us to places we never imagined.

        Our journey begins with heartfelt Telugu stories — books about love, memory and the people who shape us. More titles will join the shelf along the way.
